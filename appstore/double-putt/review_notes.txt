No account or sign-in. A fresh install opens on How to Play, three short practice holes: the ✕ at the top skips them, and Settings (the gear on the title screen) replays them. Then tap Courses, pick Daybreak, drag anywhere and pull back to aim, and tap Putt. One swing rolls both balls; Mirror flips the second ball's shot.

Double Putt is free to try: all of Daybreak, and one hole of every other course, are free. Courses has those holes as the Sampler, a round right under Daybreak; Free Play has them as its first row. One non-consumable in-app purchase, All Courses ($4.99, Family Sharing on), opens every other hole, including courses added later. To see it, tap any locked course in Courses or locked hole in Free Play, or open Settings and tap Unlock All Holes. Restore Purchase is in Settings and on the purchase screen.
