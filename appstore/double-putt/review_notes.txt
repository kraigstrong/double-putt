No account or sign-in. Tap Courses, pick Daybreak, then drag anywhere and pull back to aim, and tap Putt. One swing rolls both balls; Mirror flips the second ball's shot.

Double Putt is free to try: all of Daybreak, and one hole of every other course (the first row in Free Play), are free. One non-consumable in-app purchase, All Courses ($4.99, Family Sharing on), opens every other hole, including courses added later. To see it, tap any locked course in Courses or locked hole in Free Play, or open Settings (the gear on the title screen) and tap Unlock All Holes. Restore Purchase is in Settings and on the purchase screen.
