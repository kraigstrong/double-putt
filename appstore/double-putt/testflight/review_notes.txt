No account or sign-in. A fresh install opens on How to Play, three short practice holes (the ✕ at the top skips them). Then tap Courses, pick Daybreak, drag anywhere and pull back to aim, and tap Putt. One swing rolls both balls; Mirror flips the second ball's shot. The one in-app purchase, All Courses, opens from any locked course or hole, or Settings (the gear on the title screen) > Unlock All Holes.
