Double Putt is mini golf with a twist: every swing plays two holes at once. Line up one putt, and both balls roll. Choose Same to send them the same way, or Mirror to flip the second ball's shot. Each pair of holes is a little puzzle: find the one swing that drops both balls for a double ace. Six courses of nine pairs take you from a spring meadow to a castle moat, a desert canyon, a winter village, and a funfair with springboards, carousels and fireworks.
