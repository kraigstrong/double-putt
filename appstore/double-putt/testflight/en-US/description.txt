Double Putt is mini golf with a twist: every swing plays two greens at once. Line up one putt, and both balls roll. Choose Same to send them the same way, or Mirror to flip the second ball's shot. Each hole is a little puzzle: find the one swing that drops both balls for a double ace. Nine courses of nine holes take you from a spring meadow to a castle moat, a desert canyon, a winter village, a funfair with springboards, carousels and fireworks, a windswept seaside links, a stadium course and a space station. It's free to try: Daybreak and a hole from every course, with one purchase for the rest.
