Thanks for playing! New in this build:
- Simpler words: each hole now has two greens, and one swing plays both.
- Free Play opens right away and scrolls smoothly. The first launch after an update shows "Setting up the greens…" for a few seconds while it gets every course ready.
- Hills and dips on the green show a grid centred on them, so they're easier to read.
- The app icon has a sturdier putter.
- Every course is still open for the beta.

We'd love to hear which holes felt too hard or too easy (and roughly how many tries they took), whether any hint or mechanic was confusing, and any bugs, crashes or sound problems. Sound follows the phone's silent switch, so turn that off to hear it.

To send feedback, take a screenshot while playing and share it with TestFlight, or use Send Beta Feedback in the TestFlight app.

The beta sends anonymous gameplay counts, like which holes were finished and in how many strokes, with no names or device IDs.
