Thanks for playing! Double Putt is almost ready for the App Store, and it's free to try:
- You'll start with How to Play: three quick practice holes that teach aiming, Same and Mirror, and scoring. It's in Settings any time.
- Daybreak and one hole of every other course are free. Play them all as a round in the Sampler, right under Daybreak in Courses, or one at a time from the first row in Free Play.
- Anything locked offers All Courses, one purchase that opens all 81 holes and every course we add later. It'll be $4.99 on the App Store, and you won't be charged in TestFlight. Please play the free holes first, then unlock it if you want to keep going.

We'd love to hear whether the free holes made you want the rest, whether you'd pay $4.99 for them, whether anything about the purchase felt pushy or unclear, which holes felt too hard or too easy, and any bugs, crashes or sound problems. Sound follows the phone's silent switch, so turn that off to hear it.

To send feedback, take a screenshot while playing and share it with TestFlight, or use Send Beta Feedback in the TestFlight app.

The beta sends anonymous gameplay counts, like which holes were finished and in how many strokes, and where the purchase screen was opened, with no names or device IDs.
