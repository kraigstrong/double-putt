Double Putt is mini golf with a twist: every swing plays two greens at once.

Line up one putt and both balls roll. Choose Same to send them the same way, or Mirror to flip the second ball's shot left to right. Each hole is a little puzzle: find the one swing that drops both balls for a double ace.

• 81 holes across nine courses, from a spring meadow and a castle moat to a desert canyon, a winter village, a funfair at night, a seaside links, a stadium course and a space station
• Windmills, sliding gates, tunnels, slopes, springboards, spinning carousels, patrol robots and force fields, all moving in real time
• A guide that points each ball's way up to its first bounce. Where it stops is yours to work out
• Rounds with scorecards and medals, Free Play to practice any hole, and a trophy shelf for every double ace
• Plays on iPhone, iPad and iPhone Duo. Open a Duo like a book and the course turns to fill the screen
• No ads, no accounts, and no internet needed to play

Easy to pick up and tricky to master. Pass the phone around and see who finds the double ace first.
